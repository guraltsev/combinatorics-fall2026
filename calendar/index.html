      L1: Set notation and basic counting 2026-08-17

      Sets, cardinality, Cartesian products, and the sum rule.

      Set notation and basic counting: from ``Set notation’’ through ``The sum rule.’’

      L2: Product rule and strings 2026-08-19

      Product rule; strings with and without repetition.

      Set notation and basic counting: sections ``The product rule’’ and ``Strings: an important type of counting problems.’’

      L3: More counting techniques 2026-08-21

      Subtraction, division, permutations, combinations, and counting models.

      More counting techniques: sections ``The difference rule,’’ ``The division principle and permutations,’’ ``Unordered selections: combinations without repetition,’’ and ``Counting problems.’’

      2

      L4: Applications of combinations and combinations with repetition 2026-08-24

      Lattice paths. Repeated-letter anagrams and multinomial coefficients. Combinations with repetition; stars and bars.

      Applications of combinations and combinations with repetition: sections ``Lattice paths in the plane,’’ ``Three-dimensional paths and multinomial coefficients,’’ ``Anagrams with repeated letters,’’ and ``Combinations with repetition.’’

      L5: Review of choice models, increasing sequences, and basic binomial expansions 2026-08-26

      Choosing the correct model: does order matter and are repetitions allowed. A second bijection for combinations with repetition: increasing integers. Integer solutions with lower bounds. The binomial formulas for . Sum of all .

      Binomial expansions, Pascal paths, and Catalan numbers: sections ``A bakery problem: four kinds of choice,’’ ``Combinations with repetition and cumulative totals,’’ ``Solutions with lower bounds,’’ and ``The binomial theorem.’’

      L6: Polynomials, paths, Pascal recursion and restricted lattice paths, intro to inclusion–exclusion 2026-08-28

      Multinomial formula and polynomial expansions Coefficient extraction from powers of multivariate polynomials. Pascal’s recurrence and triangle: algebraic and combinatorial interpretation. Recursive path counts; paths through or avoiding a specified point; recursive definition; subtraction arguments.

      Binomial expansions, Pascal paths, and Catalan numbers: sections ``The multinomial theorem,’’ ``Pascal’s recurrence and Pascal’s triangle,’’ and ``Recursive lattice-path counts.’’

      3

      L7: Catalan paths and the inclusion–exclusion principle 2026-08-31

      Reflection argument and Catalan numbers. Inclusion–exclusion for two, three, and finitely many sets.

      Binomial expansions, Pascal paths, and Catalan numbers: section ``Catalan paths and the reflection principle.’’ Inclusion–exclusion and applications: from ``The geometry of overlapping sets’’ through ``Proof via indicator functions.’’

      L8: Applications of inclusion–exclusion 2026-09-02

      Characteristic functions. Conclusion of the proof of the inclusion–exclusion principle. Complement and violation-set formulations.

      Inclusion–exclusion and applications: sections ``Proof via indicator functions’’ and ``The complement and violation-set form.’’

      L9: Applications of inclusion–exclusion 2026-09-04

      Applications to bounded integer solutions and strings without repeated letters that avoid or contain specified substrings. Euler’s totient function.

      Inclusion–exclusion and applications: sections ``Integer solutions with upper bounds,’’ ``Strings with forbidden substrings,’’ and ``Euler’s totient function.’’

      4

      L10: Applications of inclusion–exclusion 2026-09-09

      Review of counting strings that avoid specified substrings. The inclusion–exclusion formula when an intersection size depends only on the number of conditions imposed. Derangements.

      Inclusion–exclusion and applications: sections ``Strings with forbidden substrings,’’ ``When only the number of imposed conditions matters,’’ and ``Derangements and fixed points.’’

      L11: Applications of inclusion–exclusion 2026-09-11

      Strings that use every symbol, surjective functions, and Stirling numbers. Graphs without isolated vertices.

      Inclusion–exclusion and applications: sections ``Euler’s totient function,’’ ``Graphs with no isolated vertices,’’ ``Counting surjective functions and full-alphabet strings,’’ and ``Connection with Stirling numbers.’’

      5

      L12: Rook polynomials and forbidden positions 2026-09-14

      Review of rook numbers and rook polynomials, including examples of their computation and use. Introduction to ordinary generating functions: a generating function records the count at value . The Cauchy product of formal power series, including a formal justification for exchanging the order of summation. Counting ways to make cents using -, -, and -cent coins. We first used separate variables to record the number of each type of coin, then substituted , , and so that the exponent records total value. We also discussed truncating the generating functions when only a fixed target value is needed.

      Rook polynomials and forbidden positions: all sections. Ordinary generating-functions note temporarily omitted.
