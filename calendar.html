  • Week 1, Lecture 1 — 2026-08-17: Riemann integration and its limitations Review: the Riemann integral; theorem+proof: continuous functions are Riemann integrable; review: continuous functions, uniformly continuous functions, modulus of continuity; theorem (statement only): a function is Riemann integrable if and only if the set of its discontinuities has Lebesgue measure zero; anticipation of definition - Lebesgue measure zero: countable covers by intervals with small total lenght; the norm L1 on continuous functions; review: definition of norm; remark: L1 is not a norm on Riemann Integrable functions because separation axiom fails; Proposition (no proof yet): continuous functions with L1 norm are not a complete metric space. Read: LN: Review of Riemann integration, Sections 1–2. Optional: Axler, Chapter 1;

  • Week 1, Lecture 2 — 2026-08-19: Countability and unbounded functions Proposition + proof: continuous functions with L1 norm are not a complete metric space. Read: LN: Review of Riemann integration, Sections 3–4. Optional: Axler, Chapter 1;
